# Templates

Each template is a working starter site you copy and make your own. Don't edit a template in place: copy it into its own folder first, so you can always go back to the original.

## local-service (coming soon)

A starter for local businesses that get customers from search: gyms, studios, trades, clinics. It's planned to include:

- Home, services and contact pages
- One page per suburb or area you serve
- A booking or enquiry form
- LocalBusiness schema, so Google and AI search know what and where you are
- A sitemap and robots.txt, ready to submit

It comes from a real client build in the course, with all of that client's copy, photos and branding removed. You put your own in.

## Using a template

Once a template is here, ask Claude:

> Copy the local-service template into a new folder called my-site and walk me through making it mine.

Claude will use your website brief (see `checklists/`) to rename pages, swap in your services and suburbs, and apply your brand. Then run taste-skill and impeccable over it, so it looks like your business and not like everyone else's copy of the template.
